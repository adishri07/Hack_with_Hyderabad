# Staging database access
Last updated: 2024-06-10
Owner: Platform team

Engineers and analysts need read access to the staging database to test queries and features.

1. Email it-support@nimbuscloud.example with your employee ID and team name.
2. IT will reply with a shared username and password within 2 working days.
3. Connect using the credentials in the email. Do not share them outside your team.
