# VPN access
Last updated: 2024-03-02
Owner: Network team

Most internal tools need the VPN.

1. Install Cisco AnyConnect from the software centre.
2. Connect to vpn-legacy.nimbuscloud.example.
3. Choose the "Nimbus-Legacy" profile and sign in with your Nimbus email.

If the connection fails, email network-help@nimbuscloud.example.
