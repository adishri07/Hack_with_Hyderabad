# Laptop setup
Last updated: 2026-07-14
Owner: IT Service Desk

Your laptop arrives pre-enrolled in Intune. On day one:

1. Sign in with your Nimbus email and the temporary password from your welcome email.
2. Set up the Microsoft Authenticator app when prompted. You cannot skip this.
3. Open Company Portal and install the "Engineering baseline" or "Business baseline" bundle, depending on your role.
4. Restart once after the bundle finishes. Setup takes about 40 minutes.

If the laptop asks for a BitLocker recovery key, do not keep restarting. Raise a ticket in the IT portal under Device > BitLocker.
